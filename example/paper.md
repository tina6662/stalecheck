# Sleep and Exam Performance in First-Year Students

## Abstract

Across 120 students, nightly sleep was strongly associated with exam score (r = .70).
Each additional hour of sleep predicted 5.99 more points on the final exam.

## Results

We analysed n = 120<!--sv:n--> students. The mean exam score was 74.5<!--sv:mean_score-->,
and 42.5%<!--sv:share_over_7h--> of students reported sleeping at least seven hours.

Sleep correlated with exam score, r = 0.699<!--sv:r_sleep_score-->,
t(118<!--sv:df-->) = 10.61<!--sv:t_stat-->, p < .001.
A simple regression gave a slope of 5.99<!--sv:slope--> points per hour.
