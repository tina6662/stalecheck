\section*{Results}
We analysed $n = \sv{n}{120}$ students; \sv{share_over_7h}{42.5\%} slept at least seven hours.
Sleep correlated with exam score, $r = \sv{r_sleep_score}{0.699}$,
$t(\sv{df}{118}) = \sv{t_stat}{10.61}$, $p < .001$,
with a slope of \sv{slope}{5.99} points per hour.
